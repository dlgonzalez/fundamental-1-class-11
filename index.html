Connecting Words

We read about a business owner and an engineer. In English, we must use these tiny words (called articles) before jobs and singular nouns. Let's master the rules.

Grammar Rules: A, An, and No Article (X)

  • Use A before a singular noun that starts with a consonant sound (e.g., a teacher, a successful business).
  • Use An before a singular noun that starts with a vowel sound (e.g., an engineer, an office manager).
  • Use No Article (X) if the noun is PLURAL (e.g., They are marketers) or for names of countries/cities (e.g., He is from Iraq).

Group Mission: Who is it?

Work in groups of 3 or 4. Follow the steps to play the guessing game.

Step 1

Student A thinks of a famous person (business owner, singer, actor, athlete).

Step 2

The group must ask Yes/No questions to find clues about the person.

Step 3

When you think you know, ask "Is it [Name]?" If you are right, you get a point!

👀 Show Example Conversation

Student A: Okay, I am thinking of a famous person.

Student B: Are they a man or a woman?

Student A: A woman.

Student C: Is she an actor?

Student A: No, she is a singer and a business owner.

Student B: Is she from the US?

Student A: No, she is from Colombia.

Student C: Is it Shakira?

Student A: Yes, it is!
